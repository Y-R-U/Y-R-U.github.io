In chapter one, there is a coming soon button that is locked. The next time user goes into that section, the button
will play the unlocking animation IF all levels in the chapter are finished and it will say 'Free Play'. This has a mix
of all of chapter one, with no objective. There are a few changes; Jon wanders around the house from time to time,
sometimes sitting at the table, NOT eating. When this happens, Garfield can swing on the vine and scratch Jon's face
when he is over Jon. Jon will be knocked backward in his chair, playing the same animation it does when Garfield
scratches the back chair leg. All times Jon is knocked out in free play, he recovers after 10 seconds. When he is
trapped in the room, he will find a way out only after a minute or after Garfield opens the door. Yes, Garfield should
be able to open the door. Garfield should be able to open and close the fridge, too, with nothing to happen.

In the main menu, there are already two buttons; one says 'chapter one' and the other says 'coming soon'. Change the
coming soon one to say 'Chapter Two: Odie and Lyman' and add another button next to it that says 'Coming soon'. The
next time the player goes to the main menu it will play the unlocking animation.

## Chapter Two

When chapter two is opened, It plays a cutscene instantly, with Jon sitting at the table talking to himself with
Garfield sitting on the table. At minimum, Jon says: "My cat may be a naughty rascal, but I will always love him, as he
is still an amazing cat. I just can't help but think a dog might have been better..." Jon and Garfield sit at the
table, bored, and the doorbell rings. Jon opens the door, seeing another human with a suitcase. "Lyman!" Jon says.
"Jon." Lyman, the other man, says. Lyman has a moustache and black hair. "I'm cold. I'm hungry. I'm weak. Take me in!"
Lyman says dramatically. Jon says in reply: "Sure, Lyman. You know my home is your home." Garfield thinks: "And my
sandbox is off limits." Jon says: "Is that all you have, the one suitcase?" Lyman answers: "Not exactly. HERE BOY!"
Odie runs in, a pale yellow dog with a black spot on his side, his nose black, his ears and tail also black. Garfield
walks away and sticks his head in the corner of the walls, thinking: "Oh, Lawsey, Lawsey, Lawsey." Lyman says: "This
is Odie." Odie jumps on the table of the house, running across and then off the edge, falling and landing headfirst.
Garfield sees and thinks: "Ten billion dogs in this world, and I get Tweedledee the wonder dummy."
The cutscene fades away, into a level select screen with ten locked level buttons, with an extra one on the side that
says 'Free play.'

**Level one:** Starts with a cutscene, similar to the ones in chapter one. Jon gives Garfield his bowl of plain
biscuits, and Garfield feels extra, extra hungry. Lyman gives Odie his bowl of plain dog biscuits, and then Jon and
Lyman sit at the table to eat steak, peas, and mashed potato on their own separate plates. The cutscene ends, with the
objectives of each level clear again, the controls from chapter one the same in chapter two. In level one, Garfield
has to eat his food, then jump on Jon and Lyman's food. They both say "Ew" and stand up, say something like "I don't
want it anymore." Then they leave the food at the table and watch TV on the sofa. Garfield eats both plates of food.
Then, Garfield has to go over to Odie, and scratch him. Odie yips, and runs upstairs into Lyman's bedroom which is the
other room next to Jon's. Garfield then eats Odie's food, and the level is finished.

**Level Two:** Starts with a cutscene, Jon and Lyman are both watching TV. Odie is running around on the table,
panting heavily. Garfield thinks: "I think I'll put this dog's lights out." Cutscene ends. Odie is now just sitting at
the edge of the table, panting heavily. Garfield must jump up on the table, and scratch Odie, knocking Odie off the
table. Odie lands on his head, and yips, running to Jon and Lyman. They both say "Naughty Garfield!" Garfield must run
away from both of them for the ten second timer, and then they go back to the telly. Odie positions himself next to
the windowsill. Garfield must jump onto the windowsill and knock the vase on him. Odie will yip again, and Garfield
must run away from Jon and Lyman again. In the small cupboard under the stairs, Garfield must open the door and knock
open the box of doggie biscuits by scratching. Odie runs into the room, always panting, and eats them. Garfield must
close the door on Odie, and he wins the level.

**Level Three:** Starts with cutscene. Garfield is eating biscuits, Odie barks at him, surprising him and making him
jump. Garfield meows loudly at him, and Odie yips off to Lyman's room. Cutscene ends. Jon is eating chicken soup.
Garfield must interact with the soup to splash it everywhere. Lyman walks out, in a white disco suit. Garfield must
walk against Lyman's legs to shed all over it. Level complete.

**Level Four:** Starts with cutscene. Garfield walks across the table, and when he's on the middle of the table it
warps down so far to touch the ground. Jon's eyes widen, who is sitting at the table. "Diet time," Jon says. Cranky at
his new diet, Garfield wants to take it out on Odie. Cutscene ends. Garfield must open jump up on the windowsill, and
open the window. Then Garfield goes over to Odie, who next to the table. Garfield goes behind him, and scratches Odie,
launching him towards the window. Odie lands on the wall below the window. Garfield thinks: "Drat. He usually lands in
the neighbour's lawn. This diet has got me weak." Odie yips and jumps onto the table. (Jon and Lyman are not in the
house, probably at work.) Garfield jumps on the table, thinking: "Maybe if I scratch him from higher he will go
higher." Garfield scratches Odie, and he is launched out the window, yipping. Level complete.

**Level Five:** Starts with a cutscene: Jon hears a knock on the door, and he opens it to find a delivery man. The
delivery man hands Jon a box and Jon says: "Oh, goodie, the new TV!" Jon puts it on the floor and opens the box,
swapping the old TV with the new TV and leaving the old TV on the floor, on the carpet. The 'new' TV should be the
same kind as the old one. Odie runs and stands next to the old TV. Cutscene ends. Garfield must search for a mouse hole
and then place cheese around the house. Then there will be a cutscene of Jon and Lyman running around trying to catch
mice as they run all around the house. With the distraction up, Garfield must walk behind the new TV and grip the
carpet. He will pull it, making the TV flying off and land on Odie, flattening him. Level completed.

**Level Six:** Starts with short cutscene. Garfield thinks: "Well, aren't I bored today." Cutscene ends. Jon and
Lyman sitting are next to each other on the couch, with Odie sitting on the floor at the foot of the couch. Garfield
has to go to Jon's room, break Jon's drawer by scratching it, and interact to pull out a spit-ball launcher. Garfield
goes back downstairs and interacts to launch spit-ball at Odie. When he does this, another cutscene begins, in which
Odie gets hit by the spit-ball, yips, surprising Lyman, who has coffee and accidentally spills it on Jon, Lyman kicks
Odie when Jon hits Lyman, and Odie bites Lyman and Lyman hits Jon, and they get caught up in a mess of fights. Cut back
to Garfield, who thinks "Much better." Level ends.

**Level Seven:** Starts with a short cutscene. Odie and Garfield facing each other off, and a notification pops up the
top of the screen, saying 'Arena Unlocked'. This level is a tutorial to the arena. It guides on how to play arena,
where each time Garfield hits Odie (scratch mechanism) Garfield gets a point. Each time Odie hits Garfield, he gets a
point. Make the battle is always in Jon's bedroom. Make Odie's AI very easy to defeat as there will be harder things
to fight in the future. First to twenty points wins. Whether Garfield wins or not, the level is completed after
someone does. The arena button will appear in the main menu screen after it is unlocked.

**Level Eight:** Starts with cutscene. Garfield drops a heap of hair on the ground and thinks: "Oh goody, its shedding
week. Time for some fun." Cutscene ends. Garfield first has to go upstairs to Jon's room and shed all over his bed.
(Shed is interact in specified areas.) Then he has to go back down and shed all over the sofa. Then he has to shed all
over the armchair. Then he has to shed all over the table. However, when shedding on table, a cutscene commences. In
the cutscene, Garfield's entire coat comes off, leaving him bald and only skin visible. Jon comes and looks, saying:
"It was bound to happen, Garfield." Cutscene and level ends.

**Level Nine:** Short cutscene to start. Garfield thinks: "Boy, am I in a bad mood." Cutscene ends. Garfield has to sit
at the table and wait for Jon to walk by. Jon says: "Goooood moooorrrning Garfield!" Garfield has to interact to poke
Jon in the face. Jon makes a painful look and walks away, saying to Lyman: "Garfield's sure been in a nasty mood
lately." Lyman replies: "We'll see about that. Do you know how to treat a mad cat?" The walk over to Garfield who is
still on the table. Garfield has to hold down the interact button to look seethingly mad. Jon looks in fright and
finishes: "With great, great respect." Level finishes with a cutscene. Odie, Jon and Lyman sneak up on Garfield,
suddenly jumping out grabbing him saying: "WE LOVE YOU GARFIELD!" Garfield's frown is turned upside down and feels
loved again, level end.

**Level Ten:** No cutscene. Garfield has to go up to Jon's room, open his sock drawer, and jump in it. Interact to play
in it, and then Odie should walk in. Garfield gets an idea and puts socks all over Odie, covering his tail, his ears,
and his mouth. Odie walks out and Jon sees the socks on him. "Garfield!" Jon shouts and chases Garfield for 10
seconds. Then Garfield finds a whistle on the floor of Jon's room, thinks "Hmm, a whistle." He must interact to pick it
up and then blow as hard as he can through it. It makes no noise, and Garfield thinks: "Must be broken." Cutscene, of
Garfield throwing it behind him and walking away, seeing Odie shaking terribly, making it clear it was a dog whistle.
Level ends.

Free play for chapter two is only unlocked after all levels in chap two are completed. Like chap one, it is a mix of
everything from the levels of the chapter. It should not include anything from chapter one unless it is in chap two.
Things that contradict can happen separately, with a given chance to happen and cancel each other out if happen at
same time, e.g. Lyman with the white disco suit.
