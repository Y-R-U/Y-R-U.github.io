# Original brief (verbatim — the child's design is the spec; don't drift from it)

Parent (Aaron): Web three.js game, mobile + desktop, 3rd person. Desktop focus, tablet next, phone based on the
tablet layout. Personal-use fan game: use the proper names and look/feel of Garfield. Allow the player to name the
main characters. Good-looking graphics, good cat and human models and animations/movement. Speech via local Qwen TTS.
Background music (YuE2 OK, non-commercial). Settings: music on/off + volume slider (default mid/low), easy fullscreen
toggle, other sensible settings. Hosted at br8t.com/mal/garfield/ (unlisted). Later addition: "lots of random/cheeky
character barks for when idle etc."

Aaron's answers: art style = **soft movie-style 3D** (rounded, warm-lit, Pixar-ish, like the 2024 film — fuzzy
orange fur, cosy house). Garfield = **voiced thought bubbles** (Jon talks aloud; Garfield's sarcastic thoughts are
bubbles AND voiced, lazy deadpan).

## Child's prompt

**Intro and menu screen:**
Starts off in a short cutscene, zooming in on the cul-de-sac Garfield lives in. Then zooming in on house. Then going
into house. In the same cutscene, Jon, is eating steak with peas and mashed potatoes at the house table. Garfield,
orange cat with black stripes, and is very fat, pounces on the food (still in cutscene.) Then, it goes to a menu
screen a locked button that says 'Chapter One', and an animation of the button unlocking plays. The cutscene is only
played on first time going on game. On the button that is now unlocked, next to 'Chapter One' it now says ': Food'.
So, in total, the button says, 'Chapter one: Food'. Another locked button appears next to the first one, saying
'Coming Soon'.

**Chapter One:**
When the Chapter One button on the main menu screen is pressed, it goes to another menu, with 10 level buttons that
are locked. On the right of the levels, there is another locked button that says 'Coming Soon'. The first one runs an
unlocking animation, and you can enter the level. Once each level is completed, the one after is unlocked.

Inside each level, the player controls Garfield from a third person view, around the 3d house. Each level in chapter
one starts with a cutscene, the same one for the whole chapter except for one detail. The cutscene is of Jon giving
Garfield his bowl of cat biscuits, and then going to eat his meal of steak, peas, and mashed potato. The food changes
for each level, levels 1, 2, 7 are the steak peas and mashed potato. Levels 3, 5, 6, and 10 is a pan of lasagna (Jon
puts some lasagna on his plate from the pan) and levels 4, 8 and 9 are plain meatloaf. Garfield takes a bite of his
own cat biscuit food and spits it out immediately, and the cutscene ends.

Design a 3d house, with a kitchen, (table, bench, and fridge in kitchen), living room (Olden day small TV in living
room, small sofa in living room, lounge chair in living room, window in living room with curtains, door leading to
outside just next to living room that can't be opened in chap one), bedroom upstairs, with Jon's bed and Garfield's
mini bed with a blanket in it.

In each level, after the cutscene, the player controls Garfield movement around the 3d house with either WASD controls
or fixed joystick in the bottom left. Jump with space or button on the middle right. Pause menu in top right corner,
with options 'Start Over', 'Exit', and 'Resume' in that order. In bottom right, there is a 'Scratch' button that
extends claw out. If it hits Jon, if on leg he hops on the other foot for a few seconds and then chases after
Garfield for 10 seconds, or until he catches Garfield. If in face he covers he face with his hands for a few seconds
and conjures a newspaper and throws it off Garfield, if it hits Garfield he is knocked back. If on his butt then he
puts his hands over his butt and then chases Garfield. If Jon catches Garfield, there is short cutscene of Jon
whacking Garfield with a newspaper, then Jon walks off, end cutscene. Nothing negative happens to Garfield, except
the delay of the cutscene. There is a tutorial in the first level for all these controls, and in the tutorial, Jon is
walking around the house before sitting down to eat his food after 30 seconds.

The aim of each level in chapter one is to steal Jon's food.
1. Garfield has to scratch Jon in the face and then press space/click on the food when standing on it.
2. Scratch one of the back chair legs of the chair Jon is sitting on; it breaks and Jon falls backwards in the chair.
   Then Garfield goes on the table and eats the food (space/click on food while standing on top).
3. (Food must match that level's cutscene.) Jump onto the windowsill and knock a vase off by scratching, making Jon
   get up and see what happened. While Jon is distracted, eat Jon's food.
4. Shred the curtains by scratching multiple times, making Jon get up to see. Then eat the food.
5. Jump on multiple obstacles to get up to the fridge. Up there is a vine Garfield clicks/spaces when very close. The
   vine swings back and forth over the table with Jon's food; press space over the food to catch the lasagna pan, and
   eat it.
6. The only way is to Scratch the food on the table, which flings it across the table onto the floor. Eat it on the
   floor.
7. Jump on the windowsill, open the window (interact while on the sill); a cold breeze floods the house. When Jon goes
   to close the window, eat the food.
8. Go into Jon's bedroom and scratch up his bed. When Jon goes in to see, interact with the door to close it, trapping
   Jon. Then eat his food.
9. Go under the table and jump several times, knocking the table up (he's so fat). Then walk under Jon's chair, which
   bounces up because he's so fat. Jump on the table, where Jon is shaking and stunned. Eat the food.
10. Extra cutscene after the first: Jon says to Garfield: "You won't let me eat my food; I won't let you eat my food.
    Fine, I just won't eat." Jon puts the lasagna pan in the fridge. Garfield jumps on the windowsill and knocks the
    vase off. Jon walks over; Garfield scratches Jon, who hops on top of the vase fragments, slips and falls face first,
    lying motionless. Garfield goes to the fridge, interacts to open it, and eats the lasagna. Level and chapter
    finished. Any level can be replayed.
