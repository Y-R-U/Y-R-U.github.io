WELCOME TO THE DISPATCH DESK

Keep it moving.

01

Earn your first company

Tap anywhere on the main scene to earn $60, then buy Golden Harvest. Owned routes start travelling continuously. Upgrade fares and expand the fleet to grow your earnings. Dispatch gives a journey a hands-on boost.

02

Hire a manager

Managers collect the full fare and keep earning while you’re away. Without one, routes still collect a smaller fare.

03

See every side of the journey

The main camera and each route show the same vehicles in the same journey. Use a route’s camera button to pin its cinematic view, or let Auto tour explore.

04

Go beyond the first mile

Choose Express or Heavy haul in Fleet planning, earn route mastery, open new regions, research better logistics and complete contracts. Eventually start a fresh company with a permanent legacy bonus.
YOUR NEXT CHAPTER

Build on your legacy

Cash, routes, regions and research restart. Your legacy bonus remains, helping the next company grow faster.
