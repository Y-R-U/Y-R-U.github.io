# Krayzee — Aaron's rulings

- D1 (2026-10-21) Radio callsigns are title + two digits ("Baroness Four-Two"). Typed names are never spoken; subtitles and livery only.
- D2 (2026-10-21) Emperor/Empress needs BOTH ~260k lifetime PP (~30 h) AND at least 10 online wins. The pure-points ladder stops at King/Queen until the wins are earned.
- D3 (2026-10-21) Once earned, the player may display ANY title they have unlocked (e.g. switch between King and Emperor), chosen in Settings. The chosen title is used on the radio and in subtitles.
  Applies in Free Flight and once Story is complete; Story missions, special events and ranked online rooms use the rank their rules dictate.
- D4 (2026-10-21) Online Court: rank by room points, highest gets the best title. NO 2x rule. The top spot is Emperor/Empress only if that player has already EARNED Emperor in their career (D2); then 2nd place is King/Queen and everyone else steps down a rung per place. If the leader hasn't earned Emperor, the leader is King/Queen and the ladder starts there. 0 PP = Baron.
- D5 (2026-10-21, defaults pending Aaron) Online: browser-hosted + small Go server; rank-gated hosting on trust; voice change in lobby/parked only; mid-join except Joust; no daily limit; home-wifi mode later; port 8018 after checking the box.
- D6 (2026-10-21) Great performance AND fantastic looks. Boot in LOW graphics, then step up quickly once the device proves it can hold frame rate (adaptive tiers; never start high and degrade on first impression). Settings can still force a tier.
- D7 (2026-10-21) Roadmap after core gameplay: more plane models (each its own model); landscape, roads, cities, water and effects made to look amazing; continuing perf work.
- D8 (2026-10-21) Later: optional WEAPONS — a story extension after the main game is finished, plus new online room options. Do not build yet; don't design anything that blocks it.
- D9 (2026-10-21) Pitch default: drag stick DOWN (toward you) = nose UP. Settings "Invert pitch" toggle flips it (default off).
- D10 (2026-10-21) Virtual stick defaults to the LEFT side of the screen; a settings toggle swaps it to the right.
- D11 (2026-10-21) Three views: 'chase', 'cockpit' (pilot's left seat, realistic) and 'center' (centre-cockpit: camera on the aircraft centreline, between the seats, panel centred — Aaron's preferred inside view). The in-game view button cycles chase → center → cockpit. Settings "Views" lets the player enable any subset (min 1): with one view the toggle button is hidden (fixed view); with chase + one inside view it is a 2-way toggle. Default: all three enabled. Key: settings.views = ['chase','center','cockpit'] (ordered); settings.view = current.
- D12 (2026-10-21) HUD markers: small edge-of-screen arrows / on-screen tags showing other planes (competitors — rivals/online players — emphasised; AI traffic subtler or optional), plus a TARGET marker (current objective: assigned runway/threshold, mission target). Settings can turn traffic markers off.
- D13 (2026-10-21) "LAND" assist button: asks the autopilot to land on the ground ahead of you — it picks the best touchdown spot roughly in front (runway/taxiway if one is ahead, otherwise a field), flies a short approach, flares and stops. It's up to the player to get near the target/runway first. Any control input cancels it (normal override rules).
