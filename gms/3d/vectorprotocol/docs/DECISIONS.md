# VECTOR PROTOCOL — Decisions (manager-owned)

Numbered, append-only. A later decision may supersede an earlier one by number.

## Aaron's brief (2026-10-19, essentials)
- **Mobile-first PORTRAIT** sci-fi squad battler, same core loop as **Heroes of Dragon Age**: collect + upgrade units, form a squad of up to **6 robots** (start with **2 slots**, more unlock through story mode), place them in formation, watch **auto-resolved** battles. Outcome = unit selection, positioning, synergies, temporary buffs — not direct control.
- Roles: **Tanks** (heavy, low-mobility, plating, energy shields, short-range/melee; anchor the front), **Archers** (sleek bipedal or hovering ranged: kinetic, railgun, precision energy bolts; back row damage bonus), **Mages** (energy support/casters: drones, beams, EMP, healing fields, area debuffs; side or rear slots). Each role has multiple rarities and visual tiers.
- Arena floor shared, left = player, right = enemy. Glowing circuit traces, **hex nodes as placement markers**. Base plates under each robot: **cyan corporate, orange rogue, purple experimental, gold legendary**. Fixed, slightly elevated angled camera across the board, clear left-vs-right.
- Sealed metallic arena: holographic barriers, vertical light strips, floating data panels, distant tiered spectator stands in cool neon. Particles: shield flares, tracers, energy bursts.
- UI on the edges: top bar = dual squad integrity meters + round counter; bottom strip = unit portraits with HP + ability icons; side = speed + auto.
- **Story mode**: maps of ~10–20 levels each, a road-like line left→right with numbered nodes. Story of **your for-hire mercenary company**: start small, earn cards/abilities, upgrade units + **company skills**. Upgrades gate progress, so at times you stop and do **arena matches / special events** to get stronger — **balance this**.
- **Landing page**: high 3D view of your zone; things have **neon names with leader lines**; tap → that feature (Arena, Story/Missions, Events, …). **Subtle movement.**
- **Most impressive = battle screens and characters.** Take time on the characters. Cards can be a view of the 3D character profile.
- Reference image: `refs/ref_battle_01.png` (more coming — check `refs/` for new files). Aaron likes HEIRFRAME's graphics (`gms/3d/heirframe/`, its look refs copied here as `refs/heirframe_*.png`).
- Manager runs up to 6 agents; usage limits will cut agents off, so everything must be resumable.

## Manager decisions
- **D1 Name/folder**: VECTOR PROTOCOL, `gms/3d/vectorprotocol/`. The company you run is a merc outfit (designer names it); "Vector Protocol" is the arena league / the in-world combat rules standard.
- **D2 Tech**: Three.js **0.180.0 vendored** at `../../lib/three/0.180.0/` (no CDN). ES modules, no build step. Classic inline boot watchdog in index.html (copy HEIRFRAME's pattern). If you vendor a new addon into `gms/lib/`, note it in your notes file (the manager must commit it — HEIRFRAME once broke live because of this).
- **D3 Orientation**: **portrait**, designed for 390×844 and 412×915 CSS px. On landscape/desktop the game is a centred portrait column (max aspect ~9:16, min ~9:21) with a dark themed backdrop; no rotate-nag on desktop; a styled "rotate to portrait" hint only on landscape *phones*.
- **D4 Battle board**: per side **2 columns × 3 lanes = 6 hex slots**. Front column = next to the centre line, back column = outer edge. Lanes run away from the camera (near / mid / far). Camera fixed, elevated ~30–40°, looking down the arena's long axis like the ref, so the centre line runs up the screen and both squads are side by side. Slight idle camera breathing + impact shake only.
- **D5 Combat model**: a **deterministic, seeded, tick-based sim** (pure JS, no three.js) that the 3D renderer plays back from its event stream. Stages have **1–3 waves** ("ROUND n/3"); squad HP carries between waves. Speed ×1/×2/×4. Each unit has a basic attack + a passive + a charged **Overdrive** ability. **AUTO on** = Overdrives fire when charged; **AUTO off** = the player taps a charged portrait to fire it (light timing play; composition still decides the fight). Inputs are recorded so a battle replays exactly.
- **D6 Roles & counters**: Tank / Archer / Mage (displayed with those names). Role triangle **Archer > Mage > Tank > Archer** (+25% dmg / −20% taken, designer may tune). Position bonuses: Tanks in front column get damage reduction + taunt; Archers in back column get +damage; Mages get a bonus in the far/near (side) lanes or back column. Targeting is lane-aware (front column shields back column until it falls; designer + sim finalise).
- **D7 Factions** (base plate colour): **Corporate (cyan)**, **Rogue (orange)**, **Experimental (purple)**. Legendary units get a **gold** plate regardless of faction. Squad synergies: 2/4/6 same-faction bonuses + role-mix bonuses (designer defines).
- **D8 Rarity**: Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary. Each rarity has visibly better models (more plating, emissive detail, effects), not just a card frame.
- **D9 Progression**: unit XP levels (battle XP + "scrap" fuel), **rank-ups by fusing duplicates/shards** (raise the level cap, HoDA style), ability levels, gear chips optional (designer's call). **Company skills** = an HQ tech tree of global buffs. **Squad slots 3–6 unlock at story milestones.** Temporary **buffs** = consumable pre-battle boosts.
- **D10 No energy timers, no real money.** Gating is power: the story difficulty curve outruns a straight-through player from roughly map 3 on, and the arena + events supply the extra power. Target: ~25–40% of play time in arena/events from map 3; whole story ~15–25 h. The sim agent proves this with a node harness that simulates players.
- **D11 Arena** = async PvP against generated rival mercenary companies near your power, ladder tiers, arena tokens, season-ish resets (local). **Events** = rotating special ops (daily resource ops, faction trials, a weekly raid boss).
- **D12 Art direction**: the battle and the characters are the showpiece. Real-time **3D, PBR + PMREM environment, bloom, a reflective glossy arena floor** (HEIRFRAME's planar-mirror approach is proven on the S22). Night-time neon palette per the ref. Robots are **procedural three.js geometry** (no external models), chunky readable silhouettes at phone size — the ref's mechs, not HEIRFRAME's slender civilians. HEIRFRAME's rig / PartBuilder / IK / materials code may be **copied** into this folder and adapted (never imported from heirframe's folder).
- **D13 Cards**: a card is a frame around a **live or cached render of the unit's 3D model**; tapping opens a 3D profile view (turntable, idle anim, abilities, stats). Portrait thumbnails are rendered from the 3D models at runtime and cached.
- **D14 2D art allowed where it helps**: the local Flux server (`mflux-queue`, http://localhost:7867, `flux2-klein-9b-mlx-4bit`) may be used for concept art, story-map backdrops, story stills or UI textures. **Not** for the units or the battle arena — those stay real-time 3D.
- **D15 Landing page**: a real-time 3D aerial view of your company's compound on a megacity platform at night. Buildings = features (Story/Operations, Arena, Events, Barracks/Squad, Workshop/Upgrades, Recruitment, HQ/Company). Neon labels with leader lines; subtle motion (slow camera drift, traffic, searchlights, blinking beacons, holo signs). The compound visibly grows as the company levels.
- **D16 One WebGL context**: a single renderer/canvas owned by `js/engine/`; 3D screens (hub, battle, profile/turntable, story map if 3D) are scenes that the app swaps. DOM UI on top.
- **D17 Quality**: tiers `?q=low|med|high`, auto-detected, **high on S22-class** (Adreno 730 / Xclipse). Target 60 fps in battle on the S22 at ~1.5 dpr. Measure every cost (draw calls, tris, ms). Same look cheaper, never cheaper look (HEIRFRAME D18).
- **D18 Saves**: localStorage, versioned, one key `vectorprotocol.save.v1`. Cloud sync (br8t account like HEIRFRAME) is a later phase.
- **D19 Never break the tree**: never import a file that doesn't exist yet; the shared tree must boot at every moment.
- **D20 Audio** is a later phase (ACE-Step/YuE2 music via Music Studio, SFX). Battle agent leaves `audio.sfx(name)` hooks that no-op.

## New refs (2026-10-19)
- `refs/ref_battle_02.png`: same scene with a **cleaner, more readable board**: the whole floor rectangle is visible with a hex-grid floor pattern, the units are larger, and the stands sit behind a back wall. Treat it as the primary framing target for the battle camera, with ref_01's mood and lighting.
- `refs/ref_fantasy_01.jpg`: a fantasy auto-battler Aaron really likes: physical octagonal stone pedestals as base plates, chunky characters, wolves and a treant (organic creatures), ruins backdrop.
- **D21 Fantasy crossover (APPROVED by Aaron 2026-10-19: "together is great")**: late in the story a rift (the Experimental-faction mystery) opens into fantasy realms. Some story maps are fought on **fantasy backdrops against NPC fantasy units** (knights, elves, minotaurs, wolves, treants). A separate fantasy sister game may come later and reuse the code and models. **Architecture rules now**: the unit system must support organic creatures (biped humanoid, quadruped, large brute) as well as mechs, keyed by a `realm: 'scifi'|'fantasy'` field; the arena must be **themeable** (a swappable environment module: scifi arena now, a fantasy ruins arena later) with the board logic shared; the data schema carries `realm` on units, maps and levels. Don't build fantasy content in Phase 0; just don't paint us into a corner.
- **D22 Sculptor agents (Aaron, 2026-10-19)**: units are built by dedicated **sculptor** agents, each owning 1–2 units: `js/actors/models/<id>.js` (model + anim overrides) and `js/fx/units/<id>.js` (signature attack/impact/overdrive VFX). The robots agent becomes kit owner + art lead: shared rig/materials/anims/gallery, the registry `js/actors/models/index.js` with stubs for every launch id, `docs/MODEL_GUIDE.md`, exemplar units, and reviewing sculptor output for roster coherence. The battle agent owns the FX toolkit the unit files call. Sculptors launch in waves of ~3 once the kit and the guide are ready.
- **D23 Unit scale**: one uniform board scale for all units (≈1.6, so a 2.05 m Bulwark shows at ~3.3 m); models keep their natural ROSTER size classes (S 1.0–1.2, M 1.4–1.7, L 1.8–2.1, XL 2.2–2.6 m). No per-role height normalisation.
- **D24 Landscape + portrait (Aaron, 2026-10-19)** supersedes D3's portrait-only column: the game should be designed for both orientations. Portrait stays the primary/most-polished layout; landscape gets its own layouts (battle: wider board framing with the HUD on the sides; hub: wider aerial; menus: two-column). Planned as its own phase after the current work; until then, new code must not hard-code portrait-only assumptions (camera fits by aspect, UI uses flexible layouts).
- **D25 Phone-feel fixes (Aaron playtest, 2026-10-19)**: action buttons must always be visible on mobile (dvh/safe-area; the Deploy button was below the fold), an easy fullscreen toggle at the top, reserved space top-right for the br8t login badge, and the hub's top-left company/story box collapses to just the number after a few seconds.
- **D24a Landscape details (manager, from docs/LANDSCAPE.md; Aaron may override)**:
  - Desktop uses wide layouts when the window is wide, with a "Portrait column" option in Settings. This replaces D3 on desktop.
  - On landscape phones the battle lanes move closer together, so units stay at least portrait size (about 133 px vs 122).
  - The landscape battle camera sits at about 30–36° (portrait is about 39°).
  - The 6 player cards sit on the left in a 2×3 grid that mirrors the formation; speed, auto and the other controls sit in a rail on the right.
  - The plan is docs/LANDSCAPE.md, and its work packages are UI-L0/L1/L2, BATTLE-L, HUB-L and QA-L.
- **D26 Battle intro camera (Aaron, 2026-10-19)**: a battle opens zoomed out and panned up to show off the arena backdrop, then pans down and zooms into the fight framing (~3 s). Strongest in landscape, milder in portrait. The sim clock starts only after the intro. A SKIP button and a DON'T SHOW AGAIN button show during it; Settings has "Battle intro: On/Off" (default On) to turn it back on. Battle owns the camera move and its API (`playIntro`/`skipIntro`/`introActive`), ui owns the buttons and the setting.
- **D27 Balance target (manager, after sim-4)**: the per-map ceiling (no single map over ~3.5 h for the smart player) beats the total-length target. The smart harness player now finishes in 9–14 h. Real players are slower than that policy, so expect roughly 15 h+. Keep focus shards (m5–m8 first clears + faction trials). The fallback lever if Aaron wants a longer game is shop shards at 150 tokens. Mutual KO on the final wave = win. m1 enemy levels eased ([1,5]) so m1_l4 isn't a wall before recruit and arena unlock.
- **D28 Barracks vs Workshop (Aaron, 2026-10-20)**: Barracks = your units (roster, squad, unit profiles; level up, rank up and ability training stay in the profile). Workshop = company fabrication with no unit grid: buff fabrication (the formation chip stays as a quick buy), the shard exchange, a scrap overview and a locked "gear chips: coming soon" bay.
