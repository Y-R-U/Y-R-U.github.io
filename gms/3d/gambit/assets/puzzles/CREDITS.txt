GAMBIT puzzle data

600 puzzles are from the Lichess.org open puzzle database
(https://database.lichess.org/#puzzles), released under CC0 (public domain dedication).
Each puzzle keeps its Lichess id and a link to the source game.

18 classic positions (famous games from 1750-1912, traditional mating patterns, and the
studies of Saavedra 1895 and Réti 1921) are public domain; move lists were re-verified with
the GAMBIT rules core.
