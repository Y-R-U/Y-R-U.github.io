# Refs (from Aaron, 2026-10-31) — a LOOSE guide, not a spec

Aaron: "a loose guide, you may have your own good ideas for how to make it look great."
Use them for mood, palette, material quality and ambition. Our default camera is near-overhead
(D2), so the environment around the board shows mostly when the player tilts the view.

## ref_battle_fantasy.jpg — battle animations (over the top is fine, they're optional)
- Fire, magic lightning, explosions, flying debris, smoke, a dragon breathing fire.
- Clear side identity even in chaos: **blue + gold (white side)** vs **red + black (black side)**.
- Cracked/scorched tiles, embers. Take: capture battles can leave short-lived scorch marks/debris
  that fade, camera shake, light flashes that colour the whole board for a moment.

## ref_fantasy_island_board.jpg — Fantasy theme board + environment
- Board on a floating stone island: gold-trimmed edge, glowing blue rune medallions at the corners,
  braziers with fire, banners, greenery, waterfalls, other floating islands/castle beyond the clouds.
- Pieces are **figurines** (robed wizards, armoured knights, rooks as towers) — ivory/gold with
  blue cloth vs black/red with red cloth. Polished marble squares with soft reflections.

## ref_gold_black_marble.jpg — premium "Royal" look (good template for Classic or a "Royal" theme)
- **Polished gold vs glossy black-chrome**, strong specular highlights, reflections in a polished
  black/white marble board with gold inlay borders and ornate gold corner filigree.
- Dragon knights, crowned queen/king with cross finial, rook battlements — every type reads
  from its top silhouette.

## UI cues (all three)
- Corner player plates: round crest medallion (lion = player, dragon = AI), name, clock, in a dark
  translucent panel with thin gold border. Captured-piece counts as small icons with numbers.
- Menu buttons as dark rounded panels with gold icons (Settings, Undo, New Game).
- Fantasy serif display type (Cinzel-like) for titles; clean sans for numbers.
