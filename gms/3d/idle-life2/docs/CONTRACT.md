## Amendments
- **Content scope** follows DESIGN.md: 12 lines and 4 districts. There is no education and there are no rides.
  - Remove `study` and `data/education.js`.
  - Plot files exist only for the 12 lines, plus `home.js` (the 6 housing tiers + family) and `bootstrap.js` (bench, cans, recycling bin). Bootstrap may live inside home.js.
- **`act` types changed:**
  - Removed: `study`.
  - Added:

    | `pickCan` | `{}` (bootstrap tap) |
    | `cashCans` | `{}` (bin tap) |

  - `family`: `{ gen, portraits:[], landmarks:[], legacy }`

### L1 P4 fixes (2026-10-04): PLAYTEST_1 economy and life
- Kids grow on life XP, not the capped age: `kid.bornXp`, `kidAge = (life.xp − bornXp)·xpYears`. Birth timing is `life.nextBirthXp`, and old saves with `nextBirthAge` are migrated on load. Kid stages are baby 0 / toddler 2 / kid 5 / teen 15.
- Age is mostly time played: `LIFE.xp.perMinute` is 0.5, and unlock 0.4, permit 0.8, housing 0.5. Homes have `minAge` 27 / 37 / 44 / 52, so life beats land on time for any play style.
- Fallback heir: `LIFE.heirAge = 60`, `data.heirNames`, `kid.apprentice`, and `life:beat {kind:'heir'}`.
- The permit gate counts finished contracts, and buying the permit auto-claims them. `quote('permit').contracts` and the `permit` act's result carry `claimed`.
- `ECON.sigmaWalkIn = 0.35`. Unmanaged lines sell σ_walk-in·P online and offline, and only the rest goes to the shelf.
- `advanceOffline` now invalidates the `stats()` cache. Before this, it returned pre-offline values until the next tick.
