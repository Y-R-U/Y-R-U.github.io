HOW TO PLAY

Drag anywhere to steer

Your rig projects a micro-singularity onto the surface. Drag anywhere on screen and it follows. Keyboard works too — WASD or the arrows.

Small things first

You can only swallow objects up to your current tier. Eat enough and the aperture widens: debris → clutter → vehicles → structures → buildings → towers → megaliths → landmarks.

Your size is your audience

Mass is only half of it. Hype multiplies your effective mass, and hype comes from being watchable — fast chains, huge single bites, near misses, catching things that are running away.

Go quiet for a few seconds and the audience drifts off, and the aperture narrows with them. Hype can only ever add to what your mass already earned — you will never shrink below what you have eaten.

Sponsor offers

Cross a viewer milestone mid-run and sponsors pitch you three upgrades. They last for that broadcast only. SUBS you earn are permanent — spend them on the rig itself.

Rivals and defences

Other clearance workers stream the same planet. Anything they eat does not count toward your quota, and if one gets 20% bigger than you it will take a bite out of you. Planetary turrets knock your audience loose — swallow them once you are big enough.
