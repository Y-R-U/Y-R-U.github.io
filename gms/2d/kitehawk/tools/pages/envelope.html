Instantaneous is the peak flight-path rate from a full-deflection pull entered vertically (gravity cancels there), attributed to the speed the aircraft was actually flying when it got there. Sustained is the largest steady stick whose specific energy is unchanged over a whole 360°. Corner is where the wing first supplies everything the stick may command — the derived turn diameter is 2v/ω there.

Everything on this page is computed by importing js/sim/ and js/data/ directly. If a number here disagrees with node tools/sim.mjs --envelope, the page is wrong, not the sim.
