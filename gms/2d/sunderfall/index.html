Destructibles — the thing that has to look expensive

Most of a level is static backdrop, but a deliberate subset of it must come apart. Every destructible needs the full chain authored, not just an intact and a gone state:

intactthe normal prop, indistinguishable from set dressing until you hit it
cracked1–2 damage states, same silhouette, spalled edges and dust
shatteringthe break frames — the expensive 6–10 frames everything hinges on
debrischunks that become physics bodies, sized so they read at gameplay zoom
settledrubble that persists, occludes, and can be stood on
  • Brick & masonry — walls, arches, buttresses. Breaks along mortar lines into rectangular chunks.
  • Rock — conchoidal fracture, no straight edges, heavy dust plume.
  • Timber — splinters and planks, burns rather than shatters.
  • Trees & bushes — hinge and topple as one piece, then settle; foliage bursts separately from the trunk.
  • Glass, crates, barrels, lanterns — cheap breakables that make the world feel reactive everywhere.
