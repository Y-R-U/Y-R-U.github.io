Totals: 1,160 items and 413 hand-written questions. Every item has 8 to 12 clues, a 1–2 sentence blurb, `difficulty` 1–3,
facts with `factsMeta`, and (where the item has one) `group`, `lookalikes` and `lname`. Every pack has `fakes`.

- `item.differences = { otherId: text }` (written both ways from `tools/c1_src/_diffs.mjs`): 115 lookalike pairs, i.e. every
  lookalike pair in snakes, spiders, jellyfish, mushrooms and bees plus all 46 identical-fact pairs from `tools/l_pairs.mjs 0`
  (now reports 0). c1_test checks that each note points to a real item and is mirrored.
- Danger packs (snakes, spiders, jellyfish, mushrooms, bees) carry `notice`. Mushrooms say "Never use this to forage…" and
  are `kidsSafe: false`. Jellyfish also say to get first aid.
- Lookalike sets include coral vs milk snake vs scarlet kingsnake, taipans vs eastern brown, funnel-web vs trapdoor vs
  mouse spider, hornet vs wasp vs bee vs hoverfly, box jelly vs moon jelly, death cap vs field mushroom vs destroying
  angel, crocodile vs alligator vs gharial, cheetah vs leopard vs jaguar, green tree python vs emerald tree boa.
- `factsMeta` carries lane A's wording templates (`ask`, `askReverse`, `askBool`, `askHigh`/`askLow`, `stmt`, `minRatio`) and
  `exclusive: false` on multi-valued facts (regions, colours, fossil sites). Packs also set `imgPrompt`, `nameImgPrompt`,
  `tfImgPrompt`. Fact types used: bool (venomous, flightless, conifer, dinosaur…), num (lengthM, massKg, legSpanCm,
  diameterKm, mohs, meltingC…), year (inventions, missions, element discovery), cat (region, danger, period, diet, origin…).
- Additive fields beyond CONTRACT.md: `pack.leakExempt` (category words a clue may use, e.g. "snake"), `pack.kidsSafe`,
  `item.lname`, `factsMeta[k].values` (allowed cat values, checked by c1_test), `factsMeta[k].type: 'text'`
  (elements `symbol`, inventions `inventor`).

## Sources and licences
- **iNaturalist** taxa (`api.inaturalist.org/v1`): taxon photos, then research-grade observations ordered by votes; only
  `cc0`, `cc-by`, `cc-by-sa`, only on the `inaturalist-open-data` bucket, `medium` size (≤500 px). Credit = observer,
  page = `inaturalist.org/photos/<id>`. Taxonomy clues (family, genus) come from iNat ancestors.
- **Wikidata / Wikipedia / Commons**: lead image (enwiki pageimage) and P18 for breeds, dishes, gems, space, body,
  inventions and the difficulty-1 animals (`wikiD1`: the curated Wikipedia photo first, so easy items get the clearest
  picture); trees and flowers use Wikipedia-first for everything (`wikiAll`). Licence and artist from Commons
  `extmetadata`; NC/ND/fair use and FAL/GFDL-only files are rejected; thumbnails at 500 px (330 px for tall images).
  IUCN status is P141. Bird, mammal and frog calls are Wikidata P51 or Commons files named after the species
  (mostly xeno-canto uploads, CC BY/BY-SA), served as Commons' mp3 transcodes; generic soundscapes are filtered out
  (the Crimson rosella "woodland" recording is gone).
- **PubChem periodic table** for all 118 elements: symbol, mass, state, type, melting point, density, discovery year;
  group and period are computed and spot-checked by c1_test.
- **NASA Image Library** (public domain) for three space items Commons lacked.
- Facts I wrote by hand (lengths, regions, danger levels, clues, blurbs) were cross-checked where structured data
  exists: Wikidata length/mass (`wdCheck`, warns at a 3× disagreement), country of origin P495 (`wdCheckLabel`), Mohs
  hardness P1088 (`wdCheckNum`). Wikidata mass/length values are often wrong or for a different life stage
  (e.g. giraffe 54.5 kg, opossum 0.16 g), so those warnings were reviewed by hand rather than auto-applied. Doubtful
  claims were reworded or dropped (e.g. Ukraine's national flower, Kenya's national bird, a "faster than a bullet" clue).

## Photo review
Every pack's first photos, and all photos for snakes and dogs, were checked on contact sheets (`tools/c1_sheet.py`).
About 120 bad photos (dead, tiny in frame, eggs, maps, cartoons, wrong species like an aardwolf skull for "skull") were
rejected via `tools/c1_src/_skip.json` (the lion now leads with a clear male lion; the impala shot is gone), and a few items got hand-picked Commons files. Body pack uses diagrams
(Gray's Anatomy, PD) instead of cadaver photos.

## Tools (shared ones are owned by C1)
- `node tools/build_index.mjs [--quiet] [--strict]`: validates `data/packs/*.json` **and `data/music/*.json`**, writes
  `data/index.json` with `path` per pack ("packs/snakes.json", "music/hits-1980s.json"), `kidsSafe`, `notice` and caps
  `{ img, audio (items + question media), itemImg, itemAudio, clues, facts, groups, lookalikes, fakes, quotes, qkinds,
  easy, kidsItems }`. Broken packs are left out and the exit code is 1. Allowed licences: CC0, PD/Public domain, PDM,
  CC BY, CC BY-SA; audio may also be "Apple Music preview…" (streamed). Local srcs: `media/…` or `data/music/…`.
  Warns on < 15 difficulty-1 entries, items without difficulty, kids packs without easy pictures; the "only 2 wrong
  answers" warning is skipped for `kids: true` packs.
- `node tools/linkcheck.mjs [packs…] [--fresh] [--max-age=H] [--concurrency=8] [--json]`: HEAD then ranged GET, checks
  the content type, caches in `tools/.linkcache.json`, flags `mirror` after two failed runs, reports per-host latency.
  Last run: 2,750 C1 URLs, all OK (iNat ~2.6 s median from here, Wikimedia ~1.3 s).
- `node tools/mirror.mjs [packs…] [--failing] [--host=H] [--url=U]`: downloads to `media/<pack>/`, JPEG ≤640 px via sips
  (never upscales), rewrites the src, keeps credits, records `media/mirror.json` which c1_build re-applies. Tested on a
  throwaway pack; nothing currently needs mirroring.
- `node tools/c1_test.mjs [packs…]`: schema, fact sanity (ranges, allowed values, licences, clue counts, kids pictures),
  known-answer checks (inland taipan venomous, Pteranodon not a dinosaur, gold = Au, tungsten melting point…), 118-element
  integrity, notices, ≥15 easy entries, and a self-test that breaks a pack ten ways. Verified to fail on a broken real
  pack (exit 1). Current: 109 checks, 0 failures.
- `tools/c1_schema.mjs`: shared validation (also used by build_index), including clue name-leak detection
  (name/alt word stems, 3-letter words whole-word) and template placeholder checks.

## How to rebuild
```
node tools/c1_build.mjs                 # all C1 packs (kids-nature is built last; it reuses their media)
node tools/c1_build.mjs snakes birds    # some packs
node tools/c1_test.mjs && node tools/build_index.mjs && node tools/linkcheck.mjs
python3 tools/c1_sheet.py <pack> out.jpg [--first] [--d1] [--only=a,b]   # contact sheet to eyeball photos
node tools/c1_skip.mjs <pack> item-id:index [item-id=N]                 # reject a photo / cap photo count, then rebuild
```
Content lives in `tools/c1_src/<pack>.mjs`; API responses cache in `tools/.c1cache/` (gitignored, about 1.8 GB, 60-day TTL; delete it to refresh).

## Known gaps
- Elements: 41 heavy/synthetic elements have no photo (none exist); FAL-licensed Commons element photos are rejected.
- Audio only for birds (52), mammals (19), frogs/reptiles (5), sea (3). Many famous mammals (tiger, koala) have no
  free, species-named recording on Commons.
- NASA preview images have no w/h (schema warning only).
- Hand facts such as "typical length" are typical adult values, not maxima; danger levels are a 4-step judgement scale.
- ~850 build warnings remain, mostly expected: hand clues dropped for leaking the name (those clues were removed
  automatically), Wikidata disagreements reviewed by hand, Commons files rejected for licence.
- `media/moments/` exists but is not mine (another lane?); mirror.mjs does not touch it.
