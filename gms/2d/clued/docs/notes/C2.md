## Rebuilding
Every pack is generated, so never hand-edit the JSON. Fix the source in `tools/c2_src/*.mjs` or the builder, then rebuild:
```
node tools/c2_build_geo.mjs      # countries, flags, capitals, currencies, languages, landmarks
node tools/c2_build_movies.mjs   # movies
node tools/c2_build_people.mjs   # people, leaders, explorers, artists, actors
node tools/c2_build_art.mjs      # paintings
node tools/c2_build_books.mjs    # books (with authors)
node tools/c2_build_quotes.mjs   # quotes
node tools/c2_build_words.mjs    # words
node tools/c2_build_history.mjs  # history
node tools/c2_build_tv.mjs       # tv
node tools/c2_test.mjs           # schema + fact sanity + self-test (exit 1 on failure)
node tools/build_index.mjs       # C1's index (validates and writes data/index.json)
node tools/c2_sample.mjs [pack…] # prints auto mc/tf questions from A's generator, so you can read the wording
```
HTTP responses are cached in `$TMPDIR/clued-c2-cache`, or in `C2_CACHE` if that is set, so rebuilds run offline and
give the same output. Delete the cache to refetch.

Every builder writes a report to `tools/c2_reports/<name>.txt` (source mismatches, dropped rows). The shared
`writePack` gate (in `c2_lib.mjs`) also writes `tools/c2_reports/gate/<pack>.txt`, which lists clues it dropped for
leaking the answer under C1's stem rule, items left with fewer than 5 clues (their ladder is removed), and images
whose licence C1 does not accept. That gate is what lets every C2 pack pass `build_index`. **Before the review,
10 of the 13 C2 packs were being SKIPPED by the index** because of clue leaks, a broken languages question and two
licences.

## Sources and how facts are checked
- **Geography:** World Bank population figures, Wikipedia's area list, Wikidata (capital, continent, currency,
  languages, driving side, calling code, flag), Commons (flags and landmark photos, licence-checked).
  Transcontinental countries carry both continents (`continent: ['Europe','Asia']`), so neither is ever offered as
  a wrong answer.
- **Movies:** Wikipedia's highest-grossing lists (by year and by decade), Wikidata for directors and dates.
  Quotes must appear on the film's Wikiquote page and be 10 words or fewer (the builder enforces this).
- **People, leaders, explorers, artists, actors:** hand lists, with birth and death years and portraits from
  Wikidata and Commons. Actors' films are checked against Wikidata cast lists.
- **Paintings:** public-domain works on Commons, with artist, date and collection cross-checked on Wikidata.
- **Books:** a hand list (`c2_src/books.mjs`) checked against Wikidata for author and publication year.
  Plot summaries and clues are written in our own words. **First lines are given only for books published before
  1930 that have a Gutenberg id**, and each one is checked against the Project Gutenberg text (43 books). The builder
  throws an error if a later book has a first line. Old translations name their translator. Two caveats: The Great
  Gatsby (1925) and Winnie-the-Pooh (1926) are public domain in the US (where the site is hosted), not everywhere;
  and The Metamorphosis has no first line because the Gutenberg translation is modern. A further 32 author
  questions (pen names and other author facts) live in `AUTHOR_QS`.
- **Quotes:** each quote must be found on a listed Wikiquote page outside a Misattributed, Disputed or "Quotes
  about" section. 127 of 133 pass and the rest are dropped (see the report). Speakers are historical figures,
  public-domain authors and public speeches. Lines from works still in copyright are kept to a few words. There are
  no song lyrics. 28 famous misquotes are true/false questions with an explanation ("Let them eat cake", "Play it
  again, Sam", "Houston, we have a problem"…).
- **Words:** each loanword's language code or name, and each eponym's surname, must appear in the English
  Etymology section of its Wiktionary entry. 6 rows were dropped. Wrong answers never include a language the word
  passed through on its way to English.
- **History:** event years are cross-checked against the Wikidata dates on each article. Five cases where
  Wikidata dates a longer process (for example the Black Death 1346–52 versus arriving in Europe in 1347) were
  checked by hand and recorded in `HAND_CHECKED`. Seven articles with no Wikidata date were checked by hand against
  Wikipedia. Generated questions: year multiple choice, closest-year number questions, timeline `order` sets (4
  events at least 5 or 20 years apart) and "which happened first?".
- **TV:** first-aired years are checked against Wikidata (Bake Off had no date there and was checked by hand).
  Questions phrase years into the prompt wherever a fact could go out of date.

## Tests
`node tools/c2_test.mjs` runs C1's `validatePack` and per-pack sanity checks:
- countries: populations and areas are positive, every country has a valid continent, 190 or more countries;
- movies: release years in range, one top-of-year film per year, quotes of 10 words or fewer with a Wikiquote source;
- people packs: birth and death years in range, lifespans possible;
- books: first lines only on pre-1930 Gutenberg books;
- quotes: every quote has a Wikiquote source;
- history: timeline answers are in order;
- flags: no disputed flag is used as a question image;
- templates are present, and kids packs have enough easy picture items.

It also runs a self-test that breaks 9 in-memory copies, one per check, and fails if any break goes uncaught. Pass
extra pack files as arguments to check them too. I tested this by giving it a copy of countries with a zero
population: it failed with exit code 1.

`build_index`: 46 packs indexed, 0 errors. The remaining C2 warnings are only "only N clues" on actors (6–7 clues
each, which is valid).

- There is no separate `authors` pack. Authors are the `author` fact on books (with a "who wrote" mc and a reverse
  question), plus 32 author questions.
- The quotes pack has 127 verified quotes, short of the ~150 target. Several well-known lines were dropped because
  their Wikiquote wording differs or they are listed as disputed. Real quotes plus misquotes come to 155 questions.
- Currencies: 73 items have no clue ladder (fewer than 5 clean clues). Every currency still works in mc, tf and
  hilo questions.
- tv has 53 shows and is weighted towards the UK and US. Neighbours is matched by its plain Wikipedia title.
