Ragdojo privacy

Release review draft · 8 September 2026

Basic play needs no login. Game progress and settings are stored in your browser. Clearing browser storage removes that device’s local save.

On games.br8t.com, optional Google or email sign-in uses Firebase. Signed-in players can store career progress and settings in Firestore and choose which save to keep when devices differ. A fight in progress is never transferred.

Purchases use hosted Stripe Checkout. Our payment server keeps your Firebase account ID, Stripe order and payment references, payment amount and refund/access status separately from editable game saves. Card details are entered on Stripe, not in the game. The current review build accepts test payments only.

Optional anonymous play statistics are off by default. If enabled in Settings, they record a random browser identifier, play starts, fight number and outcome, LIGHT/DARK mode, upgrade views and conversion. No email address, save contents, free text or full referring URL is included. We respect Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track. Statistics are limited to 90 days. Turning them off removes this browser’s statistics identifier and stops further play events.

The itch.io demo sends no game analytics or account requests. itch.io has its own privacy policy. Playing on games.br8t.com opens a separate browser origin; an itch save does not automatically transfer.

Service providers may process network and security information to deliver the game and accounts. Before public release, the operator’s business name, support contact, purchase-record retention policy and applicable customer terms must be added here.
