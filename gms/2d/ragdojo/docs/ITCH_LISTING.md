**Title:** RAGDOJO — Free LIGHT Campaign

**Short description:** Draw your punches. Launch your rivals. Climb nine bandana ranks in a ragdoll dojo scribbled on notebook paper.

## Page copy

A notebook. A stick fighter. A very bad day for everyone standing in your way.

RAGDOJO turns taps and drawn gestures into punches, flips and spectacular ragdoll
knockouts. Fight through **45 LIGHT bouts**, earn ink to train your fighter, and
climb from white bandana to the Ink Master. Finish the dojo, then take your earned
upgrades back through **BULLY replay**.

- Eight gesture specials to learn and upgrade.
- Pencil strokes, doodled spectators and unruly page hazards.
- A hand-drawn look with physics-driven knockdowns.
- Touch and keyboard controls. Play without an account.
- Local progress saved in this browser.

**Controls:** Move, jump and duck with the left stick or WASD/arrows. Tap the right
side or press Space/J/R/0 to punch. Draw gestures for specials, or use keys 1–8 for
moves you have learned. Turn your phone sideways for the best view. Controls and
animated gesture hints are available in the game.

**The main home:** [games.br8t.com](https://games.br8t.com/gms/2d/ragdojo/?from=itch).
Play the free campaign there with optional Google/email login for cloud saves.
The permanent DARK upgrade adds the existing after-dark campaign, dark moves,
THUG replay, deeper training and its own music. DARK requires completing LIGHT and
winning a BULLY run. Upgrade price/availability will be announced when ready.

This itch version contains the complete free LIGHT campaign. Purchases and account
restoration are handled on games.br8t.com. Your itch browser save stays here and
does not automatically transfer to the main site. No ads or required login.

**Music:** Suno-generated instrumental soundtrack, used with commercial-use rights.
