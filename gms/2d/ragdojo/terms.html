What is free

LIGHT and DARK are free to play: both 45-fight campaigns, their moves, music and replay runs. No login is needed to play, and there are no ads.

Support the developer

If you enjoy the game, you can support the developer with a one-time payment. As a thank-you it unlocks the CYBER and DEITIES worlds for your signed-in br8t account. It is not a subscription and there is nothing else to buy. You still open each world by playing: win a THUG run for CYBER, then a SIMULANT run for DEITIES.

Your support is tied to the Google or email account you were signed in with. Sign in with that same account on any device to restore it. Payments are handled by Stripe; your card details never reach us.

Refunds

Changed your mind? Email support@br8t.com within 14 days of paying and we will refund you in full, no questions asked, even if you have already finished CYBER and DEITIES. A refund removes access to those two worlds; your saved progress is kept, and LIGHT and DARK stay free.

After 14 days, we will still help if something is broken or the upgrade does not work for you.

This doesn't affect your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.
