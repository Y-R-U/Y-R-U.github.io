Ragdojo terms and refunds

Release review draft · 12 October 2026

Ragdojo and the br8t games are made by BR8T.COM (trading as BR8T), an independent developer in Australia. Contact: support@br8t.com.

What is free

LIGHT and DARK are free to play: both 45-fight campaigns, their moves, music and replay runs. No login is needed to play, and there are no ads.

Two ways to support

Support the developer is a one-time payment. As a thank-you it unlocks the CYBER and DEITIES worlds in Ragdojo for your signed-in br8t account, for good. It is not a subscription.

br8t Pass is a subscription, charged monthly or yearly at the price shown before you pay. While it is active it includes everything across br8t games, including Ragdojo's CYBER and DEITIES worlds and paid features added later. It renews automatically until you cancel.

Either way, you still open each world by playing: win a THUG run for CYBER, then a SIMULANT run for DEITIES.

Purchases are tied to the Google or email account you were signed in with. Sign in with that same account on any device to restore them. Prices include GST/VAT where it applies.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe's merchant-of-record service, which collects and pays the tax. Your receipt comes from Link, Stripe's checkout, and your statement may show LINK.COM*. Your card details never reach us.

Cancelling the Pass

You can cancel any time: open the upgrade panel in the game and choose Manage subscription (Stripe's customer portal), or manage the order from your Link account. Cancelling stops future renewals, and the Pass keeps working until the end of the period you have already paid for. Nothing is deleted when a Pass ends: your saved progress stays, and the paid worlds reopen if you subscribe again or support the developer.

If a renewal payment fails, the Pass keeps working for up to 7 days while the payment is retried. Update your card under Manage subscription to keep it going.

Refunds

Changed your mind? Email support@br8t.com within 14 days of paying and we will refund you in full, no questions asked. That applies to the one-time support payment, even if you have already finished CYBER and DEITIES, and to your first br8t Pass payment. Refunding the first Pass payment also ends the Pass.

A refund removes access to what it paid for; your saved progress is kept, and LIGHT and DARK stay free. Renewals are not refunded automatically, but you can cancel before the next one at any time. After 14 days, we will still help if something is broken or does not work for you.

This doesn't affect your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

Fair play

The games are provided as they are and may change over time. Please do not try to break the payment or account systems. We may update these terms; the date above shows the latest version.
