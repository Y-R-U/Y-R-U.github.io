How to Play

Goal

Fill every row, column, and 3x3 box with the numbers 1 through 9. Each number can only appear once in each row, column, and box.

Controls

  • Tap a cell, then a digit on the pad under the board. Each pad key also shows how many of that digit are still to place.
  • Digit first: tap a pad digit with no cell selected to pick it up — its homes light up, and with Fast-fill on (Settings) each tap on an empty cell drops it in wherever it's fully valid. Tap the digit again to put it down.
  • Notes pencils in candidates: turn Notes on, then tap digits to add or remove them. Placing a number wipes that candidate from its row, column and box for you. On Hard and Crazy, Auto pencils in every candidate at once.
  • Hint names the technique that forces the next digit and outlines the cell; tap it again for the digit.
  • Daily (calendar, top left): one puzzle a day, the same for everyone. Solve it on consecutive days to build a streak.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys move the selection, 1-9 place a number (or toggle a note), Backspace/Delete erases, Esc deselects, N toggles notes, H takes a hint, U undoes.

Difficulty Levels

Difficulty is set by what solving actually demands, not just how many cells are blank.

  • Basic — 50 clues, spot-the-single throughout
  • Simple — 45 clues, still singles only
  • Easy — 40 clues, singles only but sparser
  • Medium — about 27 clues; needs locked candidates or a pair at least once
  • Hard — about 28 clues; needs more than pairs — usually an XY-wing, swordfish or colouring, sometimes a triple or X-wing — but never a guess
  • Crazy — about 25 clues and no ceiling: most need chains or beyond, past anything the hint button knows

Tips

  • Look for rows, columns, or boxes that are nearly complete.
  • Use notes to track possible candidates for tricky cells.
  • Conflicts are highlighted in red and underlined to help you spot mistakes.
  • Every puzzle has exactly one valid solution.
  • Hints still count as a win, but they don't set a best time.
