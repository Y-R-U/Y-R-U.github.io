## THE THING TO UNDERSTAND FIRST — difficulty is graded, not counted
Clue count is a bad proxy for difficulty: at 22 clues a third of random puzzles
are still solvable with nothing but singles, which is why the old build's Medium
was no harder than Basic. `engine.gradeTier()` solves a puzzle the way a person
would and reports the hardest technique it forced:

  0  naked / hidden singles
  1  + locked candidates (pointing & claiming), naked / hidden pairs
  2  + naked / hidden triples, X-wing
  3  none of those finish it — chains or trial and error

`SudokuEngine.LEVELS` maps each level to { clues, minClues, minTier, maxTier }.
`carve()` removes to the clue target, then steps one cell at a time — removing
makes a puzzle harder, restoring makes it easier — hunting the requested band,
keeping the closest board it saw. `generatePuzzle()` retries up to 12 times
against a 2s deadline.

Measured over 100 puzzles per level: basic/simple/easy 100% tier 0;
medium 96% tier 1; hard 96% tier 3 (4% tier 2); crazy 100% tier 3.
Worst case 49ms — the old naive solver peaked at 362ms, a visible phone freeze.

**Tier 2 is deliberately not a band of its own.** Puzzles needing triples or an
X-wing but nothing beyond are rare; hunting one oscillates between tier 1 and 3
and costs ~45ms for a worse result. Hard and Crazy are both "beyond pairs",
separated by ~6 clues. Don't "fix" this without re-measuring.

## js/engine.js – SudokuEngine
Candidate sets are 9-bit masks (bit 0 = digit 1). `this.units` is the 27 houses,
rows first, then columns, then boxes — stepLocked() depends on that order.

generateSolution()            – filled valid grid
fillGrid(grid,row,col)        – recursive backtracking, shuffled candidates
isValid(grid,row,col,num)     – row + col + box check
masks(grid)                   – { rows, cols, boxes } occupancy bitmasks
countSolutions(grid,limit=2)  – MRV backtracking, early exit at limit
hasUniqueSolution(grid)       – countSolutions === 1
solve(grid)                   – complete a puzzle, or null. Used on load to
                                recover the solution without persisting it.
gradeTier(puzzle)             – 0..3, see above
stepSingles/stepLocked/stepSubsets(k)/stepXWing  – the graded techniques
generatePuzzle(level)         – { grid, solution, given, tier }
carve(spec, deadline)         – one generation attempt
isValidCompleteSolution(grid) – full validation of a completed board
shuffle(arr)                  – Fisher-Yates

## NOTES / GOTCHAS
- **The long press was doubly broken and the fix is subtle.** `preventDefault()`
  inside the long-press `setTimeout` is a no-op — the event has already been
  dispatched. Worse, by the time the finger lifts the picker is covering the
  spot that was pressed, so the synthesized click lands on a *number button* and
  places a digit nobody chose. The fix is a capture-phase click swallower on
  document with a 700ms deadline (`suppressClickUntil`). A latched boolean flag
  is wrong: a long press that ends outside the picker never produces a click,
  and the flag would then eat the player's next real tap.
- **The service worker sees every request its clients make**, not just files
  under its scope — including `/lib/auth/*.js` and Firebase's calls to
  googleapis.com. Cache-first on those means stale auth code and replayed API
  reads. sw.js therefore ignores anything cross-origin or outside
  `self.registration.scope`, and is network-first for HTML/JS within it.
- The solution is never written to localStorage; `loadGame()` rebuilds it with
  `engine.solve()` from the given cells only, so a tampered grid can't poison it.
- Saved games only carry the board — undo history is session-scoped by design.
- A v2 save (`sudokuGame2`) is deleted rather than migrated: its notes used the
  old positional scheme and can't be translated.
- Best times are only set by wins with no hints (`cleanWins`). A hinted win
  still counts as a win.
- Bump CACHE_NAME in sw.js when deploying.
- CSS nth-child selectors draw the 3×3 box borders without JavaScript.

## ACCOUNT LAYER (games.br8t.com)
`js/cloud.js` calls `syncLocalKeys` from `/lib/auth/localsync.js` with the three
durable keys above. `js/boot-cloud.js` imports it dynamically and swallows
failure, so the game still plays offline / blocked / from file://. `?test` skips
it entirely so headless runs stay hermetic.
`window.SudokuCloud.puzzleFinished()` is called from the win path only.
`.header-icons` shifts by `--br8t-account-space` under a 660px media query — on
a wide screen the card is nowhere near the avatar and no shift is wanted.
Read /games/CLAUDE.md before touching any of this.
